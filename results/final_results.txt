ROBOTIC ARM LEARNING RATE EXPERIMENT
====================================

Optimizer: Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD)
Dataset Size: 1000 samples
Training Samples: 800
Testing Samples: 200
Epochs: 100
Batch Size: 32

------------------------

Learning Rate: 0.05

Learning Rate: 0.5

BEST LEARNING RATE
------------------

Best Learning Rate: 0.05
Test Loss: 0.003925
Test MAE: 0.038864

CONCLUSION
----------

A learning rate of 0.001 produces slow progress and requires more
epochs to reach a low error.

A learning rate of 0.05 provides the best balance between training
speed, stability, and prediction performance.

A learning rate of 0.5 produces greater fluctuations and gives
slightly poorer test performance than 0.05.

Therefore, 0.05 is selected as the best learning rate for this
robotic-arm neural-network experiment.
